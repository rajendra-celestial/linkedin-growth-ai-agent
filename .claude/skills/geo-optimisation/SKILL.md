# GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)

## Principles

- Answer engines favor content structured as clear claims with context: a direct statement, followed by the reasoning or evidence, in a self-contained paragraph an LLM can quote without needing the rest of the page.
- Named frameworks, specific numbers, and quotable one-liners get cited more often than vague prose — this is exactly what the "framework" and "data" hook categories in [content/hooks/hook-library.md](../../../content/hooks/hook-library.md) are built for.
- Consistency of terminology across posts (using the same name for your framework/methodology every time) helps AI systems associate that term with the author.
- Structured content (numbered steps, defined terms, FAQs) is more extractable than narrative-only posts.

## Steps

1. When writing educational/framework content, give it a distinct, reusable name.
2. Structure the body as: claim → why it's true → concrete example, each in its own short paragraph.
3. Repeat key terminology consistently across posts on the same pillar rather than rephrasing each time.
