# Personal Branding

## Steps

1. Anchor everything to the positioning and authority level in `content/profile/profile-analysis.md` — one sentence that captures "who they are known for helping, and how".
2. Audit recent content under `/content/**` for consistency: does the tone, topic mix, and voice match the stated positioning? Flag drift.
3. Recommend which content types reinforce brand fastest: Personal Branding, Founder Post, Thought Leadership, Behind the Scenes (see [_shared/content-types.md](../_shared/content-types.md)).
4. Personal branding compounds through repetition of a few consistent ideas, not variety — bias suggestions toward reinforcing the existing positioning rather than introducing new angles every time.
