# Lead Generation

## Steps

1. Confirm the target persona (CEO, Founder, Marketing Head, HR, CTO, Agency, Startup Founder, Investor, SMB, Enterprise, Freelancer, Developer).
2. Write the post's pain point and proof section in that persona's language and priorities — a CTO cares about scalability/risk, a CEO cares about growth/ROI.
3. Use Sales Post, Case Study, or Problem-Solution content types (see [_shared/content-types.md](../_shared/content-types.md)) with a lead/sales-category CTA from [content/cta/cta-library.md](../../../content/cta/cta-library.md).
4. Keep the sell soft: the post should earn the right to the CTA through genuine value, not lead with the pitch.
