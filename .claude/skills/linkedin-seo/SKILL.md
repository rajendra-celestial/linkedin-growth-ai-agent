# LinkedIn SEO

## Principles

- LinkedIn indexes headline, About, experience titles, and post text for people-search — place primary keywords in the first 5 words of a headline and the first sentence of the About section.
- For posts, use 3-6 `keywords` naturally within the body (never stuffed into a keyword list) — see the `keywords` field returned by `content-writing`.
- Dwell time and comments matter more than keywords for feed reach — SEO here is about being *findable*, not about gaming feed distribution.
- Native content (text posts, LinkedIn-native documents/carousels) outranks external links. If a post needs a link, put it in the first comment, not the post body.

## Steps

1. Identify the 3-6 keywords the target ICP would search for.
2. Check the headline, About, and recent posts for keyword coverage; flag gaps.
3. Weave missing keywords into the next generated post via `content-writing`.
