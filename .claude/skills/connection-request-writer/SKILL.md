# Connection Request Writer

## Rules

- LinkedIn connection notes are capped at 300 characters — every word counts.
- Reference a specific, real reason for connecting (shared interest, their content, a mutual context) — never a generic "I'd like to add you to my network".
- No pitch, no CTA beyond the connection itself. Selling in a connection request is the fastest way to get ignored or reported.
- Match formality to the recipient's seniority/context — a peer-to-peer note can be casual, a cold outreach to a VP should be crisp and respectful of their time.

## Output

Provide 2-3 variants under 300 characters each.
