# Trend Research

## Steps

1. Identify 2-3 credible, recent developments in the user's niche (use web search if available in the current environment).
2. For each, write a one-line "why this matters to [ICP]" — the angle is more valuable than the news itself.
3. Feed the strongest angle into `content-writing` as a Trending Topic, AI News, or Industry News post (see [_shared/content-types.md](../_shared/content-types.md)).
4. Log discovered-but-unused angles as bullet points in `content/ideas/trend-ideas.md` (append, don't overwrite) so they aren't lost.

## Caution

Never fabricate a statistic, a news event, or a source. If you can't verify a claim, say so explicitly and let the user confirm before it goes into a published post.
