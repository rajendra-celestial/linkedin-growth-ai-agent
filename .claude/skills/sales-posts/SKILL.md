# Sales Posts

Maps to the Sales Post content type — see [_shared/content-types.md](../_shared/content-types.md), stored under `/content/daily`.

## Rules

- Earn the pitch: 80% of the post should be genuinely useful or interesting on its own; the offer is the last 20%.
- Prefer proof (a result, a case study, a specific transformation) over claims ("we help you grow" is weak; "this client went from 2k to 40k followers in 6 months" is strong).
- Use a sales/lead-category CTA from [content/cta/cta-library.md](../../../content/cta/cta-library.md) — soft, not pushy ("If this sounds like you, let's talk" beats "BUY NOW").
- Never fabricate specific numbers or client results — use real figures the user provides, or clearly generic/illustrative framing if none exist.
