# Audience Research

## Steps

1. Gather what's known: `Profile.icp`, `Profile.audienceJson`, and any `AnalyticsSnapshot` engagement data.
2. Look for patterns in comments/DMs the user reports — recurring questions are a direct signal for `FAQ` and `EDUCATIONAL_POST` content.
3. Segment the audience into 2-3 groups if it's mixed (e.g. "peers who engage" vs "buyers who lurk") and note that content should serve buyers even when peers are the loudest commenters.
4. Persist findings to `Profile.audienceJson` so `content-strategy` can factor them into the pillar mix.
