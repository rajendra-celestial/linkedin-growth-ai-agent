# Thought Leadership

Maps to the Thought Leadership and Contrarian Opinion content types — see [_shared/content-types.md](../_shared/content-types.md).

## Rules

- Take an actual position — a post that hedges every claim isn't thought leadership, it's a summary.
- Back the opinion with lived experience or evidence, not just assertion.
- It's fine (even good) if the opinion is polarizing within reason — engagement comes from people agreeing strongly or disagreeing and saying so, not from consensus.
- Stay consistent with the author's established positioning (see `personal-branding` skill) — a contrarian take that contradicts their prior stated views damages credibility more than it builds authority.
