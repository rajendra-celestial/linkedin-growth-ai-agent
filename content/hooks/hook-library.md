# Hook Library

Curated, hand-written LinkedIn hooks grouped by psychological trigger. Use these as a style reference when writing new hooks (via the `hook-generation` skill) — don't paste them verbatim into a real post; adapt them to the specific topic and voice.

This library grows over time: whenever a post's opening line performs well, add it here under the matching category so future generations can draw on it.

## Curiosity

- Nobody talks about this part of {topic}.
- Here's what nobody tells you about {topic}.
- I wish someone had told me this before I started {topic}.
- This one detail changed how I think about {topic}.
- There's a reason most people fail at {topic}, and it's not what you think.
- I almost didn't post this.
- This will sound obvious, but almost nobody does it.
- The part of {topic} everyone skips.
- What I learned after {number} {unit} of {topic}.
- A client asked me a question last week that I couldn't stop thinking about.

## Contrarian

- The biggest myth about {topic} is costing you money.
- Unpopular opinion: {topic} is overrated.
- Everyone is doing {topic} wrong.
- Stop doing {topic}. Here's what actually works.
- I disagree with 90% of advice about {topic}.
- {topic} is dead. Here's what replaced it.
- Hot take: your {topic} strategy is the problem.
- Most "best practices" for {topic} are actually bad advice.
- I used to believe this about {topic}. I was wrong.
- Don't build {topic} until you've done this first.

- My first client almost made me quit.

- I remember the exact moment I knew I had to change.
- Here's the story I don't usually tell.
- It took me {number} {unit} to learn this lesson.
- I turned down a $100k offer. Here's why.
